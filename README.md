# Fossling Split

A local-first group expense splitter: track who paid what, see who owes whom, settle up.
Everything stays on your device — no account, no server, no network access at all.

**🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN**

No ads, no tracking, no analytics — ever. No network access. Android auto-backup is
switched off, so nothing — least of all the payment details you note down for the people
you split with — is ever copied to a cloud backup.

**🌍 IN YOUR LANGUAGE**

Available in English and French.
